# Coordinator and student guide

## Coordinator guide

### Sign in

Use the coordinator account issued by the college administrator.

### Create an event

1. Open My events, then Create event.
2. Enter category, department, venue, organizer, date, time, capacity, deadline, and volunteer count.
3. Upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP poster.
4. Submit the event for administrator approval.

### Registrations

Open Participants to review confirmed students. Only registered students should receive participation certificates.

### Volunteers

Open Volunteer applications to approve or reject requests. After approval, assign a responsibility.

### Tasks

Open Tasks for a managed event:

1. Select an approved volunteer.
2. Enter the task title and description.
3. Set an optional deadline.
4. Review progress in the task table.

### Attendance

Use QR validation or the manual fallback. QR validation records attendance once per student and event.

### Budget, gallery, and certificates

- Set an event budget and enter expenses.
- Upload approved event images.
- Issue participant or volunteer certificates after eligibility checks.

## Student guide

### Register

Create a student account using an official college email and student ID.

### Event participation

1. Browse events or open the campus calendar.
2. Review event information and registration deadline.
3. Register, cancel if needed, or apply as a volunteer.

### Tasks and notifications

Open My tasks to update assigned tasks. Open Notifications to review unread campus updates.

### QR attendance

Open My registrations and download the QR code. Present it to an authorized coordinator.

### Certificates

Open Certificates to download available PDFs or use the public verification link.

### Feedback

Students may submit a 1–5 star rating and suggestions after participation. Anonymous feedback hides the student's identity.

### Recommendations

Open Recommendations and select interests to receive rule-based event suggestions.
